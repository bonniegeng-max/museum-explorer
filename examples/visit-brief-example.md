## On-Site Prompt

For the Harihara figure:

- **先看哪里**: compare the two sides of the headdress and body.
- **它是什么**: a combined form of Shiva and Vishnu.
- **为什么值得看**: the sculpture turns a theological synthesis into visible asymmetry.
- **再看一眼**: which side feels more ascetic, and which more royal?
- **来源状态**: identification based on the exhibition label and visit notes; object-level details should be rechecked before reuse.

## Visit Memory

- What stayed with me: the face of Jayavarman VII felt quieter than the monumental scale associated with Angkor.
- Change in perspective: I entered expecting architectural history and left remembering small differences in posture, hair, and gaze.
- Unresolved question: which damaged attributes are recoverable from comparable sculptures, and which interpretations remain uncertain?
