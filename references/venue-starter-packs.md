# Venue Starter Packs

These notes provide stable orientation, not live visitor information. Before giving a dated plan, verify current exhibitions, hours, closure days, entrances, ticketing, gallery availability, and accessibility on the venue's official site.

## Palace Museum / 故宫博物院

- Official site: https://www.dpm.org.cn/
- Stable focus: palace architecture, imperial court history, painting and calligraphy, ceramics, clocks, and specialist galleries.
- Route heuristic: choose one architectural axis and one collection theme; avoid promising that every specialist gallery is open.
- Recheck: reservation rules, entrance and exit policy, specialist-gallery tickets, temporary closures, and same-day access.

## National Museum of China / 中国国家博物馆

- Official site: https://www.chnmuseum.cn/
- Stable focus: Chinese history, archaeology, modern national history, and large temporary exhibitions.
- Route heuristic: select one permanent narrative or one temporary exhibition; do not combine the entire building into a short visit.
- Recheck: reservation identity rules, temporary exhibition access, gallery closures, entry security, and opening hours.

## Shanghai Museum / 上海博物馆

- Official site: https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/
- Stable focus: ancient Chinese bronzes, ceramics, painting, calligraphy, seals, coins, sculpture, and regional exchanges.
- Route heuristic: build a material-based route such as bronze → ceramics → painting rather than attempting every gallery.
- Recheck: which building or branch hosts the target exhibition, reservation rules, gallery availability, and transport details.

## Nanjing Museum / 南京博物院

- Official site: https://www.njmuseum.com/
- Stable focus: Jiangsu history, archaeology, decorative arts, painting, and multi-building museum experiences.
- Route heuristic: decide the primary building before arrival and include transition time between halls.
- Recheck: reservation policy, open buildings, temporary exhibitions, entrances, and accessibility routes.

## Shaanxi History Museum / 陕西历史博物馆

- Official site: https://www.sxhm.com/
- Stable focus: archaeology and material culture associated with Zhou, Qin, Han, Sui, and Tang history.
- Route heuristic: follow a chronological spine and cap a short visit at five major objects or object groups.
- Recheck: reservation release rules, paid specialist exhibitions, crowd controls, gallery closures, and entry requirements.

## Suzhou Museum / 苏州博物馆

- Official site: https://www.szmuseum.com/
- Stable focus: Wu-region culture, painting and calligraphy, scholar objects, local archaeology, and the relationship between collection and architecture.
- Route heuristic: treat the building and garden as part of the visit, leaving time for transitions and close looking.
- Recheck: branch location, reservation policy, current exhibitions, entrance route, and photography rules.

## Use Rules

1. Never present these notes as a current exhibition list.
2. Cite the relevant official page when using current facts.
3. If an official page is unavailable, say so and label substitute sources by owner and scope.
4. A lender or organizer can confirm participation, but only the receiving venue can confirm that an exhibition is currently visitable there.
